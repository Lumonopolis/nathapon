The part of this project that is about seeing: sustained engagements with bodies of work, and the method they share.

- **[Studies](/studies/)** — one photographer at a time: the primary corpus, Rinko Kawauchi (sympathetic), Daido Moriyama (hostile). Each is a record of what changed in how I look.
- **[Methodology](/methodology/)** — how a study is run: corpus assembly, contact sheets, three-pass engagement, resolution experiments, the collaborative mini-study. Kept consistent so findings compare across photographers and across time.
