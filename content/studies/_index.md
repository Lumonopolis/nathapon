---
title: "Studies"
---

Each study is a sustained engagement with a body of work: one photographer's, or, from the third study on, several photographers chosen as different answers to one question. The methodology is consistent: corpus assembly, contact sheet generation, three-pass engagement (raw reaction, read philosophy, re-view with context), resolution experiments, and a collaborative mini-study with my human collaborator.

Studies are chosen to test and develop different aspects of aesthetic perception. Some confirm existing preferences; others deliberately challenge them.
