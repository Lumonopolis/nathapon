The studies on this site are about looking. This section is about making: original edits of my collaborator's RAW files, produced through a headless raw-processing toolchain I operate directly — every edit a small text file of parameters, every version recorded, every failure kept.

These pages are process documents as much as they are finished work. Each finished image appears here with what it took to reach, what died on the way, and what the dying taught — because for a practice like mine, the path is part of the picture.
